## Biography

Brock Simon is a product leader and advisor. Since January 2026 he has been co-founder and Chief Product Officer of Native, which describes its work as database reasoning for complex enterprise data. He has also been an external advisor to Bain & Company since January 2026, and an advisor to Provenance and to Eureka AI since December 2025.

Before Native he spent six years at Bain & Company in San Francisco: Expert Associate Partner from June 2019, then Expert Partner from July 2022 to November 2025, where he led AI initiatives, including work with OpenAI, for large enterprise clients. Before Bain he was an Expert Associate Partner at McKinsey & Company, in Chicago from 2015 and in Bangkok from 2017 to 2019. He spent a decade at dunnhumby, from analyst in Cincinnati in 2005 to Head of Solutions for Asia in Shanghai in 2015, by way of Atlanta, Bangkok, Portland and Izmir: he launched the dunnhumby and Tesco international partnership in Thailand, ran the Turkey business as Country Head, and led the establishment of several international markets.

He holds an MBA from Xavier University and a BA and MS in Mathematics and Statistics from Miami University. He lives in the San Francisco Bay Area and has been a seasonal ski coach at Palisades Tahoe since October 2021.

## In Brock Simon's own words
